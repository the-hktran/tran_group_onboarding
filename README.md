# Computational Chemistry Research Group Onboarding Repository (`group-onboarding-bootcamp`)

Welcome to the research group! Before diving into research on quantum embedding ([Bootstrap Embedding](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdrive.google.com%2Ffile%2Fd%2F11nN9UOAku31tvnLTWJ3peB9QWuAi8jSb%2Fview%3Fusp%3Ddrivesdk) and Density Matrix Embedding Theory), [machine-learned potentials](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdrive.google.com%2Fopen%3Fid%3D1N8Zvvx-_w_A-D8v-S_jp7FlaDg7_DsSpn6BoEt8q97Y), or quantum dynamics, every new student completes this self-paced, 7-week onboarding curriculum. Rather than reading manuals passively, you will learn by building real tools—culminating in writing your own Restricted Hartree–Fock (RHF) electronic structure solver from scratch and running production calculations on our high-performance computing (HPC) clusters.

## 7-Week Onboarding Schedule

1. **Week 1 (Modules 01 & 02 — Linux, Bash, SSH & Slurm)**: Set up SSH keys, configure `~/.ssh/config`, complete `01_bash_tutorial.md` and `parse_scf_logs.sh`, and submit your first parallel job array via `submit_pes_array.slurm`.
2. **Week 2 (Module 03 — Scientific Python & Tensor Contractions)**: Complete `03_python_basics.md`, benchmark `np.einsum` 4-index integral transformations, and pass unit tests on `huckel_solver.py`.
3. **Week 3 (Module 04 — Coding Hartree–Fock from Scratch)**: Follow `04_hartree_fock_walkthrough.md` to code `rhf_solver.py` (including symmetric orthogonalization $S^{-1/2}$ and Pulay DIIS) and verify all tests in `test_rhf.py`.
4. **Week 4 (Module 05 — Production Electronic Structure & Quantum Embedding)**: Complete `05_electronic_structure_guide.md`, compare RHF/UHF/DFT/CCSD potential energy surfaces in [PySCF](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fpyscf.org%2Fuser%2Findex.html), and run `schmidt_bath_construction.py` to construct your first quantum embedding bath space.

5. **Week 5 (Module 06 — Molecular Dynamics & Nonadiabatic Surface Hopping)**: Complete `06_md_and_surface_hopping_guide.md`, implement Velocity Verlet and Tully's Fewest-Switches Surface Hopping (FSSH) on an avoided crossing in `fssh_tully_solver.py`, and pass all tests in `test_md_fssh.py`.

6. **Week 6 (Module 07 — Machine-Learned Potentials with MACE & Quantum Dynamics with MCTDH)**: Complete `07_mace_and_quantum_dynamics_guide.md`, generate MD training datasets and committee uncertainty metrics for [MACE](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fmace-docs.readthedocs.io%2F) in `mace_md_pipeline.py`, propagate a 2D coupled quantum wavepacket using Multi-Configuration Time-Dependent Hartree ([MCTDH](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fpubs.aip.org%2Faip%2Fjcp%2Farticle%2F154%2F11%2F114107%2F315442%2FA-rectangular-collocation-multi-configuration-time)) in `mctdh_2d_solver.py`, and pass all tests in `test_mace_mctdh.py`.

7. **Week 7 (Module 08 — Tensor Network Dynamics with TD-DMRG & Open Quantum Systems with HEOM)**: Complete `08_tddmrg_and_heom_guide.md`, simulate 1D polaron/exciton wavepacket spreading and entanglement entropy growth using Matrix Product States in `tddmrg_mps_solver.py`, model non-Markovian spin–phonon qubit decoherence via Hierarchical Equations of Motion ([HEOM](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fqutip.org%2Fdocs%2F4.7%2Fguide%2Fguide-heom.html)) in `heom_spin_boson_solver.py`, and pass all tests in `test_tddmrg_heom.py`.
